**Tamuning, Guam — March 6, 2026.** The Todu Guam Foundation Health & Wellness Center (TGF) is proud to announce that **Dr. Alan Butler, Psy.D**, Licensed Clinical Psychologist, has joined the organization's growing MindCare Mental and Behavioral Health Program, expanding access to behavioral health services for the people of Guam.

Dr. Butler joins **Dr. Danny McClure, DO**, Board-Certified Psychiatrist, as part of TGF's integrated collaborative care model, where behavioral health specialists work closely with primary care providers to deliver coordinated, whole-person care addressing both mental and physical health.

&nbsp;

![Psychiatrist Dr. Danny McClure DO](/images/news/dr-danny-mcclure.jpeg)
*Psychiatrist Dr. Danny McClure DO*

“Dr. Butler brings strong clinical expertise and a deep commitment to serving the people of Guam,” said Lena Calvo-Rodriguez, CEO of the Todu Guam Foundation. “His addition strengthens our MindCare program and advances our mission of breaking down barriers to mental healthcare access for everyone in our community.”

&nbsp;

Dr. Butler earned his Doctorate in Clinical Psychology (Psy.D) from Antioch University Seattle, an APA-accredited program, and has extensive experience providing psychological therapy, evaluations, and diagnostic assessments for children, adolescents, and adults in both community mental health and private practice settings.

&nbsp;

Dr. Butler's areas of expertise include:
* **Psychological evaluations**
* **Individual therapy for children, adolescents, and adults**
* **Couples and family therapy**
* **Diagnostic assessments** for conditions such as ADHD, Autism Spectrum Disorder, learning disabilities, and other behavioral health concerns

&nbsp;

At TGF, Dr. Butler will support the MindCare Behavioral Health Program, which provides integrated mental health services including psychiatric care, psychotherapy, psychological assessments, and collaborative treatment planning alongside primary care providers.

&nbsp;

“Our integrated model allows patients to receive coordinated care that addresses both their mental and physical health,” said Dr. Danny McClure, DO. “Dr. Butler's experience and collaborative approach will be a tremendous asset to our patients and to the continued growth of the MindCare program.”

&nbsp;

The Todu Guam Foundation Health & Wellness Center continues to lead efforts to expand healthcare access across Guam by serving insured, uninsured, and underinsured residents. The center offers a Sliding Fee Discount Program for qualifying patients, along with other financial assistance programs supported through partnerships and charitable resources, helping ensure that cost does not become a barrier to receiving care. TGF accepts most major insurance plans and provides pathways to care for individuals who may not otherwise have access to healthcare services.

&nbsp;

Individuals seeking behavioral health services may now schedule appointments with Dr. Butler through the MindCare program at the Todu Guam Foundation Health & Wellness Center.

&nbsp;

The foundation works to break down barriers that prevent individuals and families from receiving the care and assistance they need, serving insured, uninsured, and underinsured residents across Guam.
